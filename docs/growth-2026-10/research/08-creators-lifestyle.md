## How the data was gathered

- Instagram: public profile metadata (follower count, bio) and the latest grid posts with caption and date. Instagram rounds large counts (12K, 16K, 119K).
- TikTok: public profile data. Video dates were decoded from video IDs.
- YouTube: subscriber count from the channel page; upload dates from the public channel feed.
- Substack: public archive and About page. Substack shows subscriber counts in buckets.
- "Sponsored" means a disclosure tag ("AD", "Ad", "Anzeige"), a discount or affiliate code, or a brand link was seen. A "none seen" answer covers only the posts read. It is not proof.
- Not done: no engagement analysis, no audience demographics, no rate cards. Only Kiddo Adventures publishes a media kit.

## Rules for any pitch (from the product catalogue)

- No per-home valuation. Census 2021 is shown per area as a description, never ranked and never used to filter people.
- OSM layers count places, not people.
- Wildlife layers are recorded sightings (GBIF) and depend on how many people look.
- The site collects no emails today, so measure collaborations with the existing utm_source retention reports. Use a different utm_source per creator.
- A paid post needs a visible disclosure under UK ASA and CMA rules. Gifted or free data does not remove that duty if there is any incentive.
