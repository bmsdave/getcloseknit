# Close Knit — what the product offers (for audience research)

Free, map-first site about London neighbourhoods (getcloseknit.com): web PWA, a browser extension that shows area facts beside Rightmove/Zoopla/OnTheMarket listings, and a Claude MCP plugin.

- Area metrics (LSOA/MSOA/borough): crime, deprivation, income, food hygiene ratings, nature score, wildlife sightings (GBIF), price per m², typical home price, social rent, walk to station; price timeline, recent sales, property history (EPC).
- Wildlife layers (GBIF sightings): bats, deer, fox, frog, hedgehog, heron, house sparrow, kingfisher, newt, owl, swift, woodpecker. Trees/nature layers in progress.
- Places from OpenStreetMap, as map layers and comparisons: cafés/specialty coffee, pubs, bars, nightclubs, late-night food, takeaways, markets, bakeries, bookshops, libraries, museums, theatres, cinemas, gyms, yoga, tennis, sports centres, pools, playgrounds, nurseries, dentists, pharmacies, vets, dog parks, bike parking, EV chargers, coworking, universities; e.g. "specialty coffee vs kebab shops", "quiet nights".
- Schools: full pages (Ofsted, results, admission distances, London + borough comparison).
- Census 2021 tables per area (age, household, tenure, country of birth, language, year of arrival…): descriptive facts for one area only.
- Tools: Find my area (9-factor match + door-to-door TfL commute), compare areas, rankings, postcode/sector/area pages (indexed by Google), shareable links.
- Rules: no per-home valuation; census is never ranked or used to filter people; OSM counts places, not people; the site collected no emails on 2026-10-17 (the owner then chose an opt-in email digest, see ../README.md).
- Analytics: DAU, engaged time, retention by utm_source.
