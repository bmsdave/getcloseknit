Rules
  • Tags: London extremes only, against the 33 boroughs (best / worst 10%). Deprivation may be a tag (PR #94).
  • Income and social renting: descriptive rows in People, never tags, never sorted.
  • Tiles: the same 12 as the area page, borough medians; tapping one jumps to its section.
  • Chip bar: At a glance · Areas · Price · Safety · Schools · Getting around · Green & quiet · People · Living costs · Questions. Sticky under the header.
  • Areas: sort by Price, Crime, Schools or A–Z; 9 rows, then “Show all”. Phone: one card per area with three strips.
  • Rankings (old tab) is reached from each section’s London chip; Questions stays a section at the end.
Values, neighbourhood names’ figures and strip positions illustrative.
For the implementer
  • Reuse the area page’s components (header, tags, tiles, chip bar, section frame) with a borough data adapter; no borough-only layout.
  • Remove the old borough header (“Open on map” primary, ♡ + Watch row), the tabs and the “at a glance” table.
  • Width: the Task 16 container (1120). Areas list links to /area/lsoa/… pages; sort state in the URL.
