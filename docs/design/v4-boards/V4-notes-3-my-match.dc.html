Task 21d · Priority orderV4-21d-match-priority
Build
  • One ordered list, top = most important; drag ≡ (long-press on phone); new chips join at the end. Travel-to places are items too, any number.
  • Must = toggle on any chip: no soft edge, no weight, sits in a Must block on top.
  • Weights from position (rank-order centroid, w_i = 1/n · Σ_{k=i..n} 1/k); score = weighted geometric mean of fits; ties by #1’s value, then #2.
