Guide › Safety

How to read crime rates

Crime rates on Close Knit are recorded crimes per 1,000 people, over the last 12 months, from police.uk. A busy high street or station counts every visitor’s crime against the few people who live there, so centres look worse than they feel.

We compare each area with London: “More crime than 89%” means 89 in 100 London areas have fewer recorded crimes per person.

Look at the kinds of crime too. Shoplifting near a shopping centre says little about a quiet street two minutes away.

Privacy

We do not ask for your name. Searches stay in your browser unless you save a link. We count page views and clicks without cookies, and you can turn that off in ≡ › Settings.

If you send feedback, we keep the text and the page it came from for 12 months. We never sell data or share it with estate agents.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests; you can read the balancing test on its own page.
