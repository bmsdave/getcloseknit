Behaviour
1 · Snaps
  • A sheet declares which snaps it allows and where it opens; it only rests on those.
  • Same heights for every sheet; the content never sets the height.
2 · Drag
  • A press becomes a drag after 8 px.
  • Release: the nearest allowed snap. A flick (≥ 0.5 px/ms) goes on to the next snap its way.
  • Past the first or last snap: rubber band, never more than 64 px.
  • ↑ / ↓ on the handle: one snap. Reduced motion: no slide.
  • A sheet without peek closes when flicked or dragged down past half.
3 · Scroll
Peek / half: a vertical drag anywhere moves the sheet; the content does not scroll.
Full: the content scrolls. Pulled down while at the top, the sheet lowers to half.
  • Sideways rows (chips, tags) scroll sideways and never move the sheet.
  • Text stays selectable with a mouse; a finger scrolls it.
