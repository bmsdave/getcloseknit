How areas are ranked
1. Must = filter. A toggle on any chip. A Must chip has no soft edge and no weight: outside it the area is out (grey). It moves to the “Must” block on top.2. Order = importance. One list, top is most important; the user drags ≡. Crime or schools first is the user’s call. No dots, no sliders.Travel to is in the list too. Add any number of places (work, partner’s work, grandparents, gym); each is its own item with its own minutes, priority or Must. In the example: King’s Cross #3, Canary Wharf #5, Ealing #11.3. Weights from the place, non-linear (rank-order centroid, wi = 1/n · Σk=i..n 1/k): 15 ranked → 22%, 15%, 12%, 10% … 0.4%. A linear 15…1 would give #1 only 12.5% and blur 15 criteria into an average.4. Score = weighted geometric mean of each criterion’s fit (1 inside the range, down to 0 across the soft edge). Ties break by #1’s value, then #2.5. Start order from two inline questions: Kids? → schools and safety first. Drive? → No: station and walk-to places rise; Yes: road collisions and noise rise. Then the user drags.6. Live. Any reorder re-sorts the top 10 at once; moved rows show ▲▼ and the criterion that moved them; Undo in the toast (Task 10).7. Best value is a separate sort, not a weight: typical area price for the chosen home type against areas with similar scores. Area level only.

For the implementer
  • Profile: criteria are an ordered array (places / commute targets are items like any other, any number); must: true moves an item out of the weighted set. Weight = ROC of its index among the non-Must items; no stored weights.
  • matching.py: weighted geometric mean with ROC weights; tie-break lexicographic on the ranked values. Return per area the top 2 contributing criteria for the “why” line.
  • Reorder: drag on the list view and on chips (long-press on phone); re-score client-side from the cached per-criterion fits, then confirm with the API.
  • Kids / Drive answers live in the profile only to order suggestions; they are never shown on area pages or used to rank on their own.
  • Best value: new API sort key; area typical price (home type) vs median of the 50 areas with the closest score vector. Hide when fewer than 20 sales.
  • Old profiles: importance dots → order by weight desc (ties by the old order); Must stays Must.
