Crime cuts most · soften it
+ Add criterion
  • Every criterion is one range card on its own layer, built-in or added. Chips show the range and −N; a chip opens its card, never the wizard.
  • Must = soft edges at 0 (a preset, the pill lights up). Dragging a square edge handle turns Must off.
  • Inside the range = 1, across the soft edge down to 0, past it = out (grey). Score = weighted geometric mean. No 50 cut, no 0.05 floor.
  • The wizard stays the quick start: its answers only create these cards with defaults (table below).
