  • UI: the zones list moves into the My match editor as the Where card (same card pattern as places and ranges); remove “Areas you drew” from Show.
  • Drawing mode: a slim top bar (“Tap corners · n so far · Undo · Cancel”), Done at the bottom on phones; fix the “0 so far” counter while a finished zone is on screen.
  • Profile / URL: keep the same zone objects in the saved profile and #profile=v1 links; zones drawn from Show today load into Where unchanged.
  • Map: zone = thin dashed outline + light tint; outside areas take the 21e colours by score, so far-out areas read red.
