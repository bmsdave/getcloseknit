About Recent sales

What it shows

Homes sold in the last years, one dot per sale, coloured by price paid or by growth since the last sale.

Scale

Cheaper / Expensive shift the colour steps toward that end of the market; Default spreads them across London.

Sub-option pattern
  • Indented under its item with a 2 px rule; opens when the item is ticked.
  • Group label on its own line (12.5 px, muted); the control below it wraps; no left labels, nothing wider than the panel.
  • Short option names: Sold price · Growth; Cheaper · Default · Expensive.
  • Legend: one swatch row, at most 3 labels (Task 11), “No data” grey dot at the end.
  • Filters: chip groups, each with All · None (as Road collisions): Type, Tenure, New build, Years.
  • Licence and method: ⓘ beside the item name opens the explainer; the panel keeps one muted line.
  • Show › Default (Task 18) resets these to: Sold price, Default scale, every chip on, last 3 years.
For the implementer
  • One ShowSubOptions layout used by Schools, Road collisions and Recent sales; segmented controls use flex-wrap, never fixed widths.
  • Recent sales filters (type, tenure, new build, years) join MapState (Task 18) and the URL.
  • Move the licence paragraph into the layer explainer (Task 12 / 13 MapSheet); keep the one-line source in the panel.
  • e2e at 360 px panel width and 390 phone: no option’s box extends past the panel; legend is one line.
