Rules
RuleDetail
Every area gets a colourNo more plain grey for soft misses: the whole map reads best → worst for this search.
Colour = rank, not raw score7 bands by the area’s percentile among scored areas: top 5% deep blue … bottom 5% deep red, the middle near white. So the map always spreads, with 2 criteria or 15.
Must = hatchedAn area that fails a Must is out: grey hatch over its colour, never red (red means “scored low”, hatch means “filtered”).
Bottom 10Toggle next to Top 10: red pins −1 … −10 and the list, each row saying which of your criteria sink it, in priority order (✗ ≈).
Wording“Least like your search”, never “avoid” or “worst areas”: it is about this user’s criteria. No bottom list or red map on area pages, rankings or SEO pages.
PaletteRed–blue diverging (RdBu), safe for red–green colour blindness; checked day and night. Pins: ink for top, deep red for bottom.
