Workload: Docker Compose (PostGIS 18 amd64, ~8 GB DB, FastAPI, Next.js, Caddy), UK users.
Sources: official provider pages or official public APIs only. Read-only; no sign-ups.

**Notes on the figures**
- Prices are per month, **ex VAT** (UK VAT 20% on top) unless marked otherwise.
- UpCloud bills in EUR. DigitalOcean, Vultr and Akamai bill in USD. The "≈£" figures use assumed rates (€1 ≈ £0.86, $1 ≈ £0.75). They are rough; check the real amount at checkout.
- "≥80 GB fix" means adding storage to a plan whose built-in disk is below 80 GB.
- All plans listed are x86-64 (AMD EPYC, Intel or unstated). None is ARM.

## 2026 price changes and caveats
- **OVHcloud** announced price changes on 5 Mar 2026, effective 2 Apr 2026 at the end of the current contract term. The post gives VPS rises of about 44–100% and Public Cloud rises averaging 9–11%. The "VPS 2027" range above is the current offer. 12- and 24-month commitments lock in the price. Source: [OVHcloud blog](https://blog.ovhcloud.com/en/posts/pricing-evolution-of-public-cloud-bare-metal-and-vps-at-ovhcloud/).
- **IONOS and Fasthosts** advertise promotional prices for the first 3 months. Compare their regular prices instead. IONOS adds a £10 setup fee.
- **UpCloud** bills in EUR, and its Starter and Premium plans are capped at 28 billable days a month. Its London prices now match its "Global" price list; only Helsinki has separate prices.
- **Excluded:** Hetzner and Scaleway, because neither has a UK region as far as known. This was not re-checked this session.
- **Not verified:** OVH's catalog shows a £0.44 Standard-backup line next to the "included" claim on its page. Mythic Beasts does not say whether its cores are dedicated. Fasthosts backup pricing is not public.

## Fit for this workload
- An ~8 GB PostGIS database wants at least 12 GB of RAM so the working set fits in shared_buffers and page cache next to Next.js and the API. 16 GB is comfortable.
- With fewer than 4 vCPU, image builds and spatial queries will be slow.
- Disk: a 100 GB minimum leaves about 60 GB for WAL, Docker images and pg_dump space. It is safer to keep backups off the box (object storage).
