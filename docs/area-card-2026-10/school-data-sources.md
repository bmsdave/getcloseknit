# School card: data sources for matching Locrating's premium card (2026-10-15)

Goal: show openly everything Locrating's premium school card shows, except Locrating's own user reviews. This is research only; nothing here is loaded yet unless the "Have?" column says so.

**What we checked:**
- The local DB `ae-pgold` (`area_explorer`), read-only.
- The loaders: `deploy/tools/etl_schools.py`, `etl_school_performance.py`, `etl_school_demand.py`, `etl_school_distances.py`, `school_history_common.py`, `school_measures.py`.
- The raw files in `~/data`.
- The government pages linked below.

Locrating's site returns 403 to fetches, so how it defines its own figures (rank, 👍/👎, "% happy", destinations) **could not be confirmed**. The definitions below are ours.

## Loaded since (migration 056, 2026-10-15)

- **Parent View:** `etl_school_parent_view.py`, release as at 6 Apr 2026 (survey from 10 Nov 2025): 153 London schools have 10+ submissions. `load-earlier` (migration 060) adds the earlier survey, one row per school from the newest of the releases as at 1 Sep 2025 (2024/25) and 2 Sep 2024 (2023/24) with 10+: 1,206 schools; the card shows the current survey when there is one, else the earlier one (`surveyEra`), never both. 1,321 of 2,258 schools now have Parent View.
- **Workforce:** `etl_school_workforce.py`, 2025/26 census (size of workforce + DfE pupil-teacher and pupil-adult ratios), 2,235 schools. DfE's pupil-adult ratio counts teachers, TAs, other school support staff and technicians; it leaves out administrative, auxiliary and business staff (101916: adults 31.2 FTE = 21.6 teachers + 8.8 TAs + 0.79 other, of 13.61 support staff). The card says "pupils per adult (teachers, TAs and other classroom support; excludes admin staff)".
- **Financials:** `etl_school_finance.py`, CFR 2025-26 (1,260 maintained schools) and AAR 2024/25 (957 academies, with the MAT's total reserve). The card shows one return, the school's own kind. Per-pupil income and spend are left out for returns shorter than 12 months.
- **Destinations:** `etl_school_destinations.py`, KS4 (494 schools) and 16-18 (358 schools), 2023/24 leavers followed in 2024/25 (provisional).
- **Destination schools (primaries):** loaded but not shown: outdated (GLA 2016); a Good Schools Guide link instead (owner, 2026-10-16). The API no longer returns `destinations.feeders` and the card has no Destinations section for primaries; table `school_feeder_flow` stays. `etl_school_feeders.py` (migration 058), GLA London Schools Atlas Feeder_Pri2Sec, OGL v2.0, from the National Pupil Database: census 2016 (latest open, 1,562 primaries, 6,822 flows), 2015 and 2014 as history. Flows under 3 pupils are unpublished and shown together as Other. The grammar share is shown only when every published flow is to a London school whose admissions policy is known (2016: 1,089 of 1,562 primaries; 110 of them send pupils to a grammar), and as "at least" when some pupils are in unpublished flows. Grammars outside London (Watford, Chesham, Dartford) are not checked, so those primaries show no share. Former gap (#225, fixed): Nonsuch High School for Girls (URN 136795) is a London state grammar (GIAS: LA 319 Sutton, Selective, open), but the Ofsted register files it under region South East (district Epsom and Ewell); `etl_schools.py` used to keep Region = London only, which left it out of table `school` and cost 16 primaries (88 pupils) their grammar share in 2016. The ETL now selects by location (a London postcode, or within 2 km of a borough boundary, flagged `outsideLondon`), so it and boundary schools such as Tatsfield Primary are loaded.

## Recommended build order

1. **Quick wins from data already held (S each):**
   - gender and trust in Details/Pupils, from the census file and the full GIAS columns;
   - first-choice applications vs places, and the % not offered;
   - honest Strengths / To-improve counts from the stored inspections.
2. **England national rank (M):** the files are already on disk. Rule as above, with N and the year always shown. London percentiles stay for the /10 ratings.
3. **Parent View (M):** a small ODS file; "% happy" (Q1) and "% recommend" (Q10), with N ≥ 10, the as-at date, and post-Nov-2025 answers only.
4. **Workforce (M):** two school-level EES CSVs.
5. **Secondary destinations (M):** KS4 (2024/25, just published) and then 16-18.
6. **Financials (M–L):** CFR and AAR, with the trust caveat; ship maintained schools first if AAR mapping drags.
7. **Catchment widening (L, legal):** keep showing only publishable LDO; send the RPSI requests in parallel (`admissions-legal.md`).
8. **Primary → secondary destination schools: do not build.** No open source exists; a primary's Destinations row shows "No data" with "Where pupils go next: not published for primary schools".

Every new section needs a source line, licence, and year in `sources[]`, and must be omitted (not zero-filled) when a school has no row. An empty row says **"not loaded"** when the active release holds no rows of that source at all (e.g. before migration 056's loads); only when the source is loaded may it say the publisher left the school out.
