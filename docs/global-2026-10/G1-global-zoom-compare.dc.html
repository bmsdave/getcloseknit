4 · Where the global map starts · options
A · City-first (recommended)
Open on Europe at city level, 60–80 EU/UK cities with Urban Audit + GHSL + sale prices where open. Countries and regions as context.
Cost: 4–5 weeks. Matches the “where do I move” question and our London depth.
B · World countries first
Choropleth of ~190 countries, ten metrics. Fast, but it is Numbeo-lite and says little about where to live.
Cost: 2 weeks.
C · Second neighbourhood market
Paris to London depth (IRIS, DVF, INSEE, police). Proves the stack travels; no global map.
Cost: 6–8 weeks.

Rules on the board
  • A metric shows only on levels where it is comparable; elsewhere the chip is struck out with a reason.
  • Cross-country crime is homicide only. Never a crime index from surveys of opinion.
  • Money never changes data or order: partners only in Next steps.
  • Out of scope here: rankings of whole countries as “best”, travel prices (no open source; flag).
