# Verification: London property-specific factors

- **1. Lease-length discounts (16% / 10% / 8%), dispersed valuer priors** - supported. NBER w20133 (Giglio, Maggiori, Stroebel) gives exactly 16% (80-99 yrs), 10% (100-125), 8% (125-150) vs freeholds; priors "very dispersed"; discounts "very similar outside of London". Note: estimates are England & Wales-wide transaction data (pre-2014), not London-specific.
- **2. Marriage value still payable; rates consultation 7 Sep-21 Oct 2026; 2027 earliest** - supported. GOV.UK gives 7 Sep-21 Oct 2026 and says provisions cannot commence until primary-legislation fixes; Which? says marriage value still payable and earliest implementation 2027. Caveat: Which? cites a 23 Sep 2026 closing date, conflicting with GOV.UK's 21 Oct.
- **3. London service charges £2,801, +41.2% in 5 yrs; 37% exceed 1%; lenders excluding** - partially. Landlord Today supports £2,801 and +41.2% for London, but 37% over 1% of value is England & Wales-wide (up from 29%), not London; "some" lenders tightened criteria. The Negotiator gives only national figures (£2,300, +34%).
- **4. BSA thresholds £325k London vs £175k elsewhere; cladding blocks hard to sell despite EWS1** - partially. GOV.UK confirms thresholds (they apply to non-cladding defect costs). MSE thread is a single anecdote with EWS1 where cladding is one of several confounders (district heating, shared ownership, main road, cheaper competition).
- **5. EPC price effect small (A/B +1.7%, F/G -3.5% vs D); rentals EPC C by 1 Oct 2030** - supported. Nationwide figures and 1 Oct 2030 PRS deadline (England and Wales) both confirmed. "Primary landlord criterion" is the researcher's inference.
