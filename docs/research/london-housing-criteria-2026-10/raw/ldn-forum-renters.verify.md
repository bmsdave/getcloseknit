# Verification: Forums - London renters

- **1. Zone 2 relist: 150 applications, 22 offers, winner +GBP 250/mo; ~40 at viewings; offers due within hours** - supported. Mumsnet 4714753 (WoolyMammoth55, Jan 2023): Zone 2 flat relisted, 150 applications, 25 viewings, 22 offers, let to someone offering GBP 250/mo over asking. Thread 4647001 (OP, Oct 2022): "40 people" per viewing, offers due "within literally hours". Both are single anecdotes, not a measured norm.
- **2. Location judged by door-to-door minutes / station / line; fares budgeted with rent** - supported. Thread 5133661 cites door-to-door times, walks to stations and specific lines, but says nothing about fares. Thread 5291692 explicitly says to add travelcard costs to rent when comparing areas.
- **3. 6-12 months upfront for no-UK-credit arrivals; RRA from 1 May 2026 caps advance rent at 1 month, bans above-advertised bids, hits intl students, tightens referencing** - partially. The 6-12 months figure and the 1 May date, one-month cap, bidding ban and impact on international students are all supported. LandlordZone does not clearly say referencing has tightened. It says only that some landlords are less willing to take risks now Section 21 is gone, and it points to guarantors. The year 2026 is implied rather than stated in the fetched text.
- **4. Agencies require ~30x monthly rent; modest earners pushed to SpareRoom flatshares** - partially. One poster (MSE 6276531) reports roughly 30x for a room let through an agency. That is one anecdote, not "typically". In MSE 6521798 nobody mentions a 30x rule or SpareRoom. There, one reply suggests a shared house and the OP rejects that option.
- **5. Victorian conversions transmit talk/smells through timber floors, cold/damp; purpose-built quieter, cheaper to heat** - partially. Noise and smells are well supported: MSE 6262743 has SaintJudy on hearing conversations and weed smoke, Doozergirl on seeing through floorboards, and Alan2020 on concrete floors. Mumsnet 4792573 has one poster on noise, damp and heating in period rentals versus warm new builds. However, both threads include counter-views, so "repeatedly" overstates how settled this is.
