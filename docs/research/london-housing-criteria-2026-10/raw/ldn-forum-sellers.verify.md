# Verification: Forums: sellers and agents on valuation

1. **GetAgent 2026 survey, 95% / 89%** — supported. PIE (9 Sep 2026): 95% of agents have seen rivals give unrealistic valuations; 89% lost an instruction to a higher valuer.
2. **Largest agencies overvalue most, ~2x commission; a third of listings cut by avg £23,400 (6.9%)** — supported. Which?: the 10 worst overvaluers charge on average double the commission of the 10 most accurate. The cut figures come from Times analysis that Which? cites.
3. **London asking-to-sold gaps: SW £82,100, NW £80,299, W £59,192; 89,582 sales; 2019** — supported. Negotiator, 12 Jun 2019, sales over the previous 12 months.
4. **London flat down-valuations rising (large blocks, thin or inflated comps); family homes near good schools hold up better** — supported. MPA Mag quotes a broker (OPortfolio). This is one broker's view, not market data. The MSE threads are anecdotes.
5. **Service charge, lease, cladding and outside space can outweigh comparables; Sydenham flat £465k asking to £325k sale, service charge £1.5k to £6k** — supported. Mumsnet seller: marketed in the mid-£400ks in 2018 and accepted £325k in 2020, with service charge £1.5k, then £3k, then £6k. The other threads cite all four factors. "Outweigh comparables" is the researcher's reading.
