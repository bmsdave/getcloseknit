# Verification: acad-hedonic-uk

1. **Leasehold discounts (80-99y ~16%, 100-125y ~10%; valuers think the discounts are smaller)**: supported. Giglio/Maggiori/Stroebel NBER w20133, p.9, says this word for word for England and Wales. It also gives 8% for 125-150y and 3% for 150-300y. The Bracke et al. LSE paper loads but is a separate PCL/Lonres study; the figures come from the NBER paper.
2. **JLE/DLR stations +9.3pp; 1.5-5.8% per km within 2 km; no effect beyond 2 km; tube access worth more than school quality**: supported. Gibbons & Machin CEP dp0611 gives the 9.3pp diff-in-diff, 5.8% per km (Table 1) and 1.5% per km (most conservative), with no effect beyond 2 km. A quarter-SD cut in station distance gives 1.5%, against under 0.75% for a quarter-SD rise in primary school performance.
3. **England: 1 SD of value-added or prior attainment gives ~3%; Scotland: only raw attainment is capitalised**: partially supported. The England half is correct (Gibbons/Machin/Silva SERC DP18). For Scotland, Rossi (2021) finds no effect of value added and ~3% per 1 SD of average attainment. But composition (% of pupils not on free school meals) is also capitalised, at ~4% per 1 SD. The paper's conclusion is that attainment and composition are "equally capitalised", so "only raw attainment" is wrong.
4. **London: criminal damage 1/10 SD = ~1% (£2,200); burglary has no measurable effect**: supported. Gibbons, "The Costs of Urban Property Crime", is about Inner London, and the abstract and conclusion match these figures. The "disorder signal" reading is the author's own explanation.
5. **EPC: A/B +5% vs D, +14% vs G; not significant in the South East; smaller in London; Nationwide +1.6% owner-occupied vs +12.2% buy-to-let**: supported. Fuerst et al. find +5% for A/B vs D, with lower or non-significant premia in London and the South East. The DECC 2013 report finds +14% vs G, no significant premium in the South East, and a significant but much smaller effect in London. The Nationwide page gives 1.6% (owner-occupied) and 12.2% (buy-to-let).
