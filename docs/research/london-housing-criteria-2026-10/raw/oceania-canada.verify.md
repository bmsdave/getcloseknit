# Verification: Sydney, Melbourne, Toronto, Vancouver

1. **Auction 0.3% expected premium; failed-auction stigma 2.6% (NBER) / 1.3% (RoF)**: supported. The NBER abstract gives a 2.6% discount after a failed auction. The UNSW page (Review of Finance, NSW/VIC, 2007-2019) gives 1.3% below private treaty, a 0.7% premium for a successful auction, and 0.3% net once failures are counted.
2. **VIC: single-figure reserve 7 days before auction from 1 Oct 2026; PPS lists 3 comparables within 6 months/2 km (Melbourne)**: partially. The figures match CAV. The reforms start on 1 Oct 2026, but reserve publication applies only to auctions held from 16 Oct 2026. Outside Melbourne the limits are 18 months and 5 km.
3. **~70% of Toronto offer-date listings in July 2023 had no accepted offer; 2026 forum consensus is that list-low backfires (HouseSigma anchoring)**: partially. The Globe says over 70%, with only about 17% accepting an offer and 53% getting no bids. The Reddit thread could not be fetched, so the forum-consensus and HouseSigma part is unverified.
4. **Sydney catchment premium up to $1.3m (39.8%); 6 of 7 premium zones grew less over 15 years**: partially. $1.3m (39.8%) is supported (Killara HS / Willoughby Girls / Lindfield LV cluster: 126.0% vs 150.3% over 15 years). However, the 6-of-7 count covers 9 clusters across Sydney and Melbourne combined, not Sydney alone.
5. **GTHA 2025 new-condo sales 1,599 (lowest since 1991); ~10% of registered presales taken back; $1,123 vs $856 psf**: supported. About 10% is roughly 3,000 units. $856 psf is the Q4 2025 resale figure.
