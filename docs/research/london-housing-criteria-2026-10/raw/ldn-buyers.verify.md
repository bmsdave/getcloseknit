# Verification: London buyers — search criteria

1. **Portal search data, max-price filter, greenspace demand** — supported. Datta, Kulka & Wehbe (Warwick TWERP 1628): 16bn searches 2019-2024 (Great Britain, not London only); ~60% of searches have a max-price filter; users update it with 25% probability per search step; greenspace effect "returned to zero by the end of 2023"; prices "overstate the persistence".
2. **Leasehold service charges** — partially. £2,801/yr London, +41.2% in 5 years, and the 50%-likelier-to-sell figure (<=1% vs >=2%) are correct (Landlord Today). But the 37% of flats over 1% of value is for England and Wales, not London (up from 29% five years earlier). "Primary London buying criterion" is the researcher's framing, not a sourced claim.
3. **School catchment premium** — supported. Santander: 5% outside London (£21,215), 25% in London; 63% of parents paid or would pay extra (26% in 2019). Gibbons, Machin & Silva (SERC DP18): 1 SD of value-added or prior achievement raises prices by about 3% (England primary schools).
4. **Transport priority / station proximity** — supported. Transport rose from 14th (2021) to 6th (2024) and square footage fell from 2nd to 7th, but in a UK-wide survey (412 buyers), not a London one. Gibbons & Machin (CEP DP611), South East London DLR/Jubilee: estimates of 1.5% to 5.8% per km within 2km.
5. **First-time buyers** — supported. Hamptons: a record 48% of London sales in H1 2024, average £443,550. London FTBs: neighbourhood 79%, bedrooms 48%, transport 43%, outdoor space 43%. Nationally 73% of FTBs buy houses (65% in 2015).
