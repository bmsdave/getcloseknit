# Verification: Migrants (South Asian and African communities in London)

1. **Home ownership by ethnicity, England 2021-23**: supported. The GOV.UK page covers Apr 2021 to Mar 2023 and gives Indian 68%, White British 70%, Bangladeshi 37% and Black African 22% (Pakistani 55%, all households 65%).
2. **Co-ethnic premium in a London location model**: supported. Haque et al. (JTRG) estimated an error-components mixed logit for owners and private renters. Preference for ethnic/racial similarity is "positive and statistically significant" for both groups, and both are willing to pay more for a higher share of same-ethnic households. The sample excludes social renters.
3. **Indian and Pakistani descendants move into ownership more than natives (ONS LS 1971-2011)**: supported. The abstract says the descendants of Indian and Pakistani immigrants "are even more likely to move to homeownership than the native population."
4. **About half of Indian buyers want vastu compliance and treat it as a deal-breaker**: supported. Camilla Dell of Black Brick says "Around half of Indian buyers search for vastu-compliant properties", and for them it is a deal-breaker. The quote is in a passage that also mentions non-resident Indians, but the source does not clearly say "especially non-resident".
5. **Tenancy refusals and Right to Rent**: supported. Generation Rent's 2024 survey of 1,058 renters gives 12.5% of minority-ethnic renters refused a tenancy vs 6.3% of white British/Irish renters. Shelter's 2024 survey of 2,000 landlords found 24% believe they cannot rent to people with a non-UK passport.
