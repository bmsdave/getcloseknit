# Verification: London location factors

- **1. Station premium (London 8% / GBP 42,700; Manchester 4.9%, Glasgow 4.6%; 9.7% in 2020-21)**: supported. PIE/Nationwide gives London 8.0% (~GBP 42,700), Manchester 4.9%, Glasgow 4.6%. The Nationwide 2021 report gives London 9.7% (~GBP 46,800) for Apr 2020-Mar 2021. The later report says London is now down from 2021 but "broadly similar to pre-pandemic levels".
- **2. DLR/Jubilee causal evidence (9.3pp; station access worth ~2x school performance)**: supported. CEP dp0611 gives a raw diff-in-diff of ~9.3pp (exp(.089)-1). It reports +1.5% for a quarter-SD cut in station distance versus "under 0.75%" for a quarter-SD rise in primary school performance.
- **3. School premiums (Savills top decile +28%; causal ~3%/SD; only top ~10% or oversubscribed schools)**: supported. Savills via PIT: top 10% of areas +28% vs regional average. The 10-20% band is +13% and the 20-30% band +15%, so the raw premium is not confined to the top decile. Gibbons-Machin-Silva (JUE 2013): 1 SD of value-added *or* prior achievement adds ~3%. LSE eprint 2939: "only the best one in ten schools" raise nearby prices, and prices are higher near popular, over-capacity schools.
- **4. Crime (criminal damage ~1% per 1/10 SD; burglary no effect)**: supported. CEP dp0574 abstract: a one-tenth SD decrease in criminal damage density adds 1% to price. Burglaries have no measurable impact.
- **5. Location shocks (ULEZ -3-4%; Heathrow -4-7%; Kew/Richmond still expensive)**: partially supported. The ULEZ part checks out: the Warwick-Monash student paper (McLoughlin 2025) finds expansion areas fell ~3-4% over the following year, with ~2% spillover. The Heathrow figures are discounts after runway announcements: -4.27%, -4.14%, -7.37% and -6.39% (ERES 2019 preliminary results). The abstract does not frame them as homes "newly entering noise contours", and an +8.96% reversal followed the August 2017 opposition announcements. HACAN supports the Kew/Richmond point (homes often sell for GBP 1m+ despite the noise). Mumsnet is anecdotal only.
