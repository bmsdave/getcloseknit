# Verification: migr-europe-americas

1. **Ukrainian arrivals: rental barriers (49% no guarantor/references, 42% can't afford)**: supported. ONS gives 50% facing barriers, 49% and 42%. Survey ran 15 to 22 April 2024; the bulletin came out 3 June 2024.
2. **North Americans 19% of international buyer registrations in Q1 2026; Europeans 54%; 5% target PCL vs 13% in 2013**: supported. The source says "more than double" a decade ago (up from 15% in Q1 2025). The 13% was the 2013 market peak.
3. **Post-2004 EU accession-born have 61% private renting (the highest); 17% of arrivals within 5 years own their home**: supported. The source says highest "among regional groups". The 17% applies to England and Wales.
4. **A 1% immigrant inflow lowers house prices about 1.6% via high-wage natives moving out; driven by low-education migrant areas**: supported. Sá (2011), IZA DP 5893, UK local authorities: 1.6% reduction, natives at the top of the wage distribution leave, effect driven by areas with lower-educated immigrants.
5. **Newcomers without a UK credit file fail referencing; workarounds; scores don't transfer**: unreachable. Both StackExchange URLs were blocked for WebFetch and curl, so nothing was verified.
