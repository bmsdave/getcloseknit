# East Asia claims: verification

- **1. Tokyo walk-time non-linearity**: supported. Diewert et al. (CIGS PDF) give -0.35%/min for 2-8 min and -2.01%/min for 8-13 min walks to the nearest subway station (land value, Tokyo).
- **2. Beijing school premium**: supported. The paper reports 4-8% (municipal KPS) and 2-3% (district KPS). The ~30% figure is one estate agent's 2013 elite-vs-other gap, cited via Xinhua 2016.
- **3. Singapore COV**: partially. Stacked says COV is never covered by an HDB or bank loan and must be cash, and it cites a 2021 headline, "1 of 3 HDB resale flats now include COV". It does not state the CPF valuation cap. HardwareZone posters (Aug 2022) report S$0 to S$90k.
- **4. CPF pro-rating to age 95**: supported. CPF Board says the rule took effect on 10 May 2019 and CPF use is pro-rated by how far the lease covers the youngest buyer to age 95. PropertyNet adds that the HDB loan-to-value is also pro-rated.
- **5. Japan rental refusal and upfront costs**: supported. Village House gives 39% and e-housing gives 39.8%, both from the 2016 government (MoJ) survey. realestateguide.jp gives 4-6 months' rent upfront, including 1-2 months of key money.
