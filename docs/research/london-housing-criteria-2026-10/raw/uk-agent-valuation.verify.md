# Verification: UK estate-agent valuation method

- **1. TPO Code mandates comparable evidence** — supported. TPO sales code 4b: any advised asking or selling price "must be given in good faith", must reflect current market conditions and "must be supported by comparable evidence"; 4c: comparables must be retained on file. (The lettings code is softer: "preferably by comparables".)
- **2. Overvaluing is widespread and costly** — supported. GetAgent: 95% of agents have seen rivals give unrealistic valuations (89% lost instructions to them). Which?: 19% of homes cut by 5%+, up to £4.3bn a year, 64 extra days to sell. Times/Which?: the 10 worst overvaluers charge on average double the commission of the 10 best.
- **3. Correct pricing: 21 vs 47 days, 63% vs 32%, ~96% of list** — partially. 21 vs 47 days compares correctly priced with over-priced homes (Rightmove, 300k listings). The 63% vs 32% is homes with no reduction vs at least one reduction, not correct vs incorrect initial price. The ~96% comes from one English agency's 1995-98 sample (CESifo WP 778: 96.2%), not all English homes.
- **4. PPD gaps, EPC floor-area join** — partially. GOV.UK confirms PPD has no floor area or bedrooms and a 2-week to 2-month lag. The EPC "net habitable area differs from floor plans" point rests only on reader comments on the PIE article, not on the article itself or an official source. "London £/sqft depends on the EPC join" is the researcher's inference.
- **5. Leasehold discounts** — partially. NBER w20133: 80-99yr leases ~16% below freehold (13% with extra fixed effects). This covers all property in England & Wales, not London flats specifically. Short-lease (<=60yr) flats: 11% below average in London, 22.1% nationally (supported). Service charge: flats at <=1% of value were 50% more likely to find a buyer than those at >=2%; 14% of flats exceed 2% (supported).
