# Verification: Paris and southern/western Europe

1. **Paris F/G +1.1%; national F/G flats -13% vs A/B** — partially. The figures (+1.1%, -13%) and the historic-building explanation are in the source. But the source is a press release from April 2022 using data since 2019, not 2022 sales. It also says "cachet de l'ancien" (old-building character) and never names "Haussmannian". In 2021, Paris F/G prices fell -4.2% vs -0.2% for other homes.
2. **Paris rent control -5–5.2%, 8.2% in 2023/24, no durable supply effect, 43.2% over cap, double with full compliance** — supported. Apur: -5% and -5.2% in the two models, -8.2% for 07/2023–06/2024, no durable effect on supply, 43.2% of listings over the cap, and an effect "more than twice" as large under full compliance.
3. **Barcelona: listings -22.2%, flat size about -5%, 462 vs 103 enquiries per listing, €/m² flat, asking rents +12%** — supported. idealista gives -22.2% listings, an average flat of 71.4 m² (nearly -5%), 462 vs 103 enquiries (Q4 2025) and €/m² "practically unchanged". Spanish Property Insight gives declared €/m² about +1% and asking rents of €21.18 → €23.73/m² (+12%). The Monras paper was not checked.
4. **DPE ban on new leases: G from 2025, F from 2028, E from 2034; F/G rent freeze** — partially. service-public confirms G from 1 Jan 2025, F from 2028 and E from 2034 (renewals too). Neither cited page mentions the F/G rent freeze, which has applied since Aug 2022 under the Climate law. boursedescredits only says investors are avoiding poorly rated homes.
5. **Portugal golden visa: about 60% more bunching at €500k, premium over fiscal value €38–50k** — supported. IZA DP16857 reports about 60% more bunching mass ((3.1-1.9)/1.9). The premium is about €38,000 on average in the text, with a 49,047 coefficient in another DiD specification.
