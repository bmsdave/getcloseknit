# Verification: migr-east-asia-hk

1. **UKHK 2022 survey (n=1,081): 31.3% own UK property, doubled; 44.1% (<1 yr) own; 62.4% consider buying in 2 yrs** — supported. OCR of the image-only PDF confirms 1,081 responses, "After one year, 15.3%" doubled to 31.3%, 44.1% of arrivals under a year already owned, and 62.4% considering buying in the coming 2 years.
2. **BN(O) families paid up to 12 months' rent in advance; many bought, some with cash before arriving; address needed for school place** — supported. Brown et al. 2026 (Soc. Sci. 15(6):385) report six to twelve months paid upfront because of no UK credit or job, cash purchases including a house bought before arriving, and "Without the address, we couldn't apply for school". Caveat: the interviews are from the North of England, not London. (MDPI blocks direct fetch, so the text was read from the mdpi-res.com PDF.)
3. **HK owners are the largest foreign group: 24,759 E&W homes worth £10.8bn (2023); 25,972 London homes (2024); Barking & Dagenham 31%** — supported, with caveats. All figures match the Benham & Reeves data in the cited articles. The 31% figure comes from the 2022 study (17 boroughs), not 2023/24. Barking & Dagenham is outer-east London, not inner-east. "Largest" is framed as most valuable portfolio (£10.8bn vs Singapore £7.2bn/15,725).
4. **Vancouver: addresses ending in 4 sell 2.2% cheaper and 8 sell 2.5% dearer where the Chinese share exceeds 18%; 2025 E&W study finds no number-13 discount after controlling for geography** — supported. IZA DP7484 gives 2.2% and 2.5% for census tracts above the 18% average. SSRN 5101113 ("The Lack of Superstition in the Housing Market in England and Wales", 2025) finds the raw 13 discount is entirely confounded by geographic supply.
5. **East Asian agencies steer clients off home-market criteria; Japanese agency: many homes 50+ yrs, don't insist on new; Korean New Malden agency: gazumping, lift-replacement levies, damp under fresh paint** — partially supported. The Able page says it word for word. The Naver post (Seoul Estate / 서울부동산, lettings4u) describes a seller switching to a higher bidder (gazumping, though the term isn't used), a lift-replacement levy hidden from the buyer, and damp/mould hidden by fresh paint. It does not name New Malden; it says only "London".
