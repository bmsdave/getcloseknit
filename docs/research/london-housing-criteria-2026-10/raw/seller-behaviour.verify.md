# Seller pricing behaviour: verification

1. **LSE CEP dp1568 (2007 peak vs 2001, ~20m sales, Zoopla list prices)**: supported. The paper says ~20m sales, a 5% price premium and a 50% lower chance of selling, and finds loss-aversion effects on list prices using Zoopla listings.
2. **Rightmove May 2026 (127 vs 36 days, 32% reduced)**: supported. All three figures match the source.
3. **Zoopla HPI (London ~30%, Scotland ~75%, northern England ~50%)**: partially supported. London 30% and Scotland around three-quarters (Apr-Jun 2026) are confirmed. The page gives no ~50% figure for northern England; it only says those regions are faster.
4. **GetAgent/PIE 89%/95%; NBER w11053 3.7% and ~10 days**: supported. The source says 9.5 days longer, which rounds to ~10.
5. **Orange County anchoring (8.1%/5.5%/5.2%); 17 Polish cities**: supported. Both match: Agarwal/Seiler/Siebert CESifo WP 10069, and Kokot/Doszyn (GUS), where offer price is the only significant explanatory variable.
