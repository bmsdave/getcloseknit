# Verification: International buyers, relocation agents and prime London

- **1. Non-doms: 57% in London, 73% of tax; 73,400 in 2024-25** - supported. HMRC: 57% located in London, 73% of non-dom income tax/CGT/NICs; 73,400 in tax year ending 2025, prior to the 6 April 2025 regime change (final publication in this form).
- **2. Thinning, not exodus; leavers keep houses; occupiers rent** - supported. KF: leavers "not selling their houses"; Savills: fewer new instructions partly from non-doms keeping their London foothold; KF lettings: HNW buyers defer and rent for 12-24 months, citing the end of non-dom status.
- **3. Condition premium ~30% sales, 31% lettings** - supported. Savills: immaculate homes about 30% above those needing upgrade; immaculate lets at a 31% premium over moderate condition.
- **4. Gulf 25% of £15m+ (20% in 2024); domestic 49%; North Americans ~10%** - supported. PIE: Gulf 25%, up from 20% in 2024; Savills: domestic 49% of central London sales, North Americans "almost 10%".
- **5. PCL 22-26% below peak; first quarterly rise in four years (Sep 2026)** - supported. KF: -22% since August 2015 peak, +0.3% quarterly in September 2026, first in four years; Savills: 26% below 2014 peak.
