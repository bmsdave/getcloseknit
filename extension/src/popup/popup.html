Open a listing on Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket, SpareRoom or OpenRent to see its area: crime, stations, parks, schools, noise and how the asking price sits against recent sales.

Sending the listing's coordinates and price is off in Settings, so Close Knit shows nothing.

What is sent

For a listing you open: its site, id and link, location, postcode, asking price, bedrooms, type and floor area, and your saved search if you chose to use it here. Costs, lease, EPC and price history are worked out on this device. Never photos, descriptions or anything about you.

Close Knit is independent and not affiliated with these sites.
